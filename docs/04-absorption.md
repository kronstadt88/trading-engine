# Absorption

Absorption is a relevant structural phenomenon that may precede or accompany a start.

It must not initially be reduced to a textbook rule such as "high volume and low range". The first engine will primarily operate on OHLCV, but the methodology is visual/structural and may later be enriched with order-flow data.

Known discussed examples:

- Heineken: bearish structure with absorption
- Euro historical example: absorption/start context

Absorption is useful evidence, but it is not currently defined as mandatory for every valid trade setup.
